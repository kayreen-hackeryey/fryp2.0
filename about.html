02. WHERE IT STARTED

ONE
DROP
AT A TIME.

Used cooking oil is part of everyday life. But once the frying is over, what happens next?

FryPals was created to explore a more useful second life for used cooking oil. Instead of treating it as something with no value, we transform it into sustainable liquid soap while encouraging more responsible waste habits.

What started as a student-led idea grew into experimentation, prototyping, research, entrepreneurship, and community engagement.
