01. OUR STORY

WHAT IF
WASTE
HAD A SECOND LIFE?

FryPals is a youth-led initiative turning used cooking oil into something useful.

Instead of allowing used cooking oil to become waste, we explore ways to transform it into sustainable products while encouraging people to rethink the value of everyday waste.
